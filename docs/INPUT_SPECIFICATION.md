## Validation notes

Invalid or duplicate timeframes, trading frames excluded from scanning, invalid TP ladders, incompatible MTF thresholds, invalid distance/risk values, and disabling the mandatory retracement are rejected during initialization. Native MT5 compilation, broker execution, and Strategy Tester validation are still required.
